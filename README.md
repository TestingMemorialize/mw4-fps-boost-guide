## 🔍 Common Performance Issues in MW4

Here is a breakdown of the issues players are facing and what causes them:

1. **Shader Compilation Stutter**
   Every time the game loads a new effect, weapon, or map, it compiles shaders on the fly. This causes brief but severe stutters. While MW4 uses *Advanced Shader Delivery* (ASD) technology, it often fails to cache properly if your drivers are outdated or your cache is corrupted.
   
2. **CPU Bottlenecks in Multiplayer**
   MW4 is heavily CPU-bound in large multiplayer maps. Background Windows processes, power-saving modes, and unnecessary services can steal CPU time from the game, resulting in inconsistent frame times.

3. **Frame-Time Spikes**
   Even if your average FPS is high, sudden spikes in frame time (the time it takes to render a single frame) make the game feel choppy. This is often caused by improper GPU scheduling or conflicting overlays.
